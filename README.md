OpenUtau Web (V1 BETA)

Status: BETA 
Current version: V1 BETA

OpenUtau Web is a touch-friendly UTAU editor that runs entirely in your browser, with nothing to install. It is a total recreation of OpenUtau V2, based on openUTAU Mobile.

What it does
Piano-roll editing: write notes and lyrics, with pitch curves and vibrato.
Classic UTAU voicebanks: import a voicebank .zip with an oto.ini. Imported singers are saved in your browser, so they’re there next time.
Projects: open and save .ustx files, including each track’s renderer choice.
Per-track settings: name, color, singer, renderer, and expressions (Gender, Tension, Velocity, Voicing).
Mixer: volume, pan, mute and solo per track, plus per-track effects (EQ, compressor, reverb).
Editing tools: quantize, transpose, find and replace, set lyrics, auto legato, fix overlapping notes, add or remove tail notes, and reset pitch bends, vibratos and expressions.
Audio export: render and export your song.
Options: light, dark or follow-system theme, page-turning and scrolling behavior, and pitch label display.
Rendering
A built-in resampler works out of the box.
WORLDLINE-R and MORESAMPLER2 are supported through optional WebAssembly modules. They are in progress; until the modules are added, the app uses the built-in resampler.
Limitations (Beta)
UTAU voicebanks only. DiffSinger, ENUNU and NEUTRINO banks are rejected.
Expect bugs and missing features while V1 is in beta
